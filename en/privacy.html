Privacy
policy.

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Cashkhak is a local tool for keeping track of cashback categories. We try to collect as little data as possible — ideally none at all.

01

Data we process

Cashkhak does not require an account and does not collect personal data. Bank card names, cashback categories and settings are stored locally on the user's device.

When a user selects an image for recognition, the app receives only that image through Apple's PHPicker. It does not request full photo library access.

02

Data sharing

The app does not send user data to a server, use advertising networks or track users across apps and websites.

The widget receives only a local snapshot of the current month's data through the App Group so it can display it on the device.

03

Website analytics

If you allow analytics, this website uses Google Analytics to measure page views and clicks on App Store links. Google receives information about your visit and may use analytics cookies. Card and cashback data from the app is not sent to website analytics.

You can change your choice under Analytics settings at the bottom of any page.

04

Contact

For privacy questions, email farakshin@gmail.com.
