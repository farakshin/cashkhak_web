Do I need an account?＋

No. Cashkhak works without an account or a server. Your cards and categories stay on your device.

Which banks are supported?＋

The directory includes around 60 Russian banks. You can also add another bank manually.

How does screenshot recognition work?＋

The app uses Apple Vision Framework to find category names and percentages in a selected image. You can review and edit the result before saving it.
